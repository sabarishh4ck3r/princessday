Princess Forever

Every princess deserves a crown, but you were born with one.

You are not just a princess in my dreams,

You are the poetry behind my silent screams.

Not because you wear a crown of gold,

But because your heart is worth more than the world can hold.

If the stars could listen, I'd tell them your name,

If the moon could understand it would glow just the same.

You deserve a kingdom, a thousand skies above,

And a lifetime filled with kindness, happiness and love.

A beautiful chapter in my life's story.

And if I could make one wish come true,

It would be to see the world be gentle to you.
